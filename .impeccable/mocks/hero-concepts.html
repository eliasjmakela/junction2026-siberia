Three ways the hero can form

Drag the slider through the 8 steps, switch the role and status, or press Play. Each concept starts vague and resolves as the company answers. In all three the finished image is unique to the role, so it can also mark the role on the event page.

1 · Constellation

A night sky full of noise. Each answer pulls one star out of the noise, places it and links it. The rest of the sky dims. The finished constellation is labelled with the role, like a star chart.

Why: signal out of noise is the challenge itself (AI sameness vs. a clear role). An event page becomes a sky of constellations.

2 · Print registration

The shape printed as three misaligned screen-print passes, ghosted and doubled. Each answer pulls the passes closer until they lock into one solid print of the role's form.

Why: comes straight from the Finnish poster world. "Coming into register" is a real printer's moment of clarity.

3 · Poster tiles

A 3 × 3 grid of blurred blanks. Each answer prints one tile as a flat geometric mark chosen by that answer (team size, start, skills…). The centre tile is the status.

Why: the image is literally made of the answers, so two roles never look alike. Reads like a 1960s pattern print.
